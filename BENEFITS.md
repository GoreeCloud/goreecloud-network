# GoreeCloud Network — Benefits

This file describes benefits supported by the current product direction and verified repository capabilities. Planned capabilities are not treated as present-day benefits until implemented and validated.

## Privacy and ownership

- Keeps the private-network control plane under GoreeCloud administration rather than requiring a proprietary hosted service.
- Removes or disables inherited analytics, attribution, advertising-ID, and commercial tracking paths that are unnecessary for GoreeCloud.
- Preserves first-party control over product direction, client behavior, administration, and long-term migration.

## Security and access control

- Uses a mature encrypted networking and WireGuard foundation while GoreeCloud replaces product-level features deliberately.
- Supports individually identifiable people and devices instead of relying on shared identities.
- Establishes private-by-default, deny-by-default, and least-privilege access as the governing model.
- Separates ordinary user/device operation from administrative authority.
- Preserves explicit enrollment, approval, ownership, revocation, groups, policies, posture, and resource boundaries.

## Reliability and migration

- Retains proven routing, NAT traversal, signaling, relay, and tunnel behavior during the fork-to-native transition rather than replacing critical networking primitives prematurely.
- Keeps inherited compatibility paths available while first-party Conduit replacements are implemented and validated.
- Requires controlled migration, backup, recovery, upgrade, and rollback before the incumbent production environment is displaced.

## User and administrator experience

- Provides dedicated GoreeCloud web and Android product foundations rather than depending permanently on upstream-branded clients.
- Moves administration toward a focused GoreeCloud private-network experience with Devices, Access Policies, Groups, Posture, Resources, DNS visibility, People, Activity, and Settings.
- Uses Glaze UI direction and GoreeCloud terminology while preserving protocol-sensitive behavior.
- Removes unrelated MSP, distributor, marketplace, billing, and support surfaces from the primary GoreeCloud workflow.

## Platform integration

- Integrates with GoreeCloud Identity, DNS, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Monitor, Manager, and other approved Suite components while preserving clear authority boundaries.
- Keeps networking focused on connectivity and access rather than duplicating reverse proxy, DNS, identity-provider, password-management, or application-authorization responsibilities.

## Long-term benefit

GoreeCloud Network is designed to gain the maturity of an established private-networking foundation while progressively increasing GoreeCloud's ownership, independence, privacy, recoverability, and ability to improve the product without being permanently constrained by an upstream vendor's commercial priorities.
