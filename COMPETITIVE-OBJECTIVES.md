# GoreeCloud Network Competitive Objectives

NetBird, Tailscale, Headscale, ZeroTier, WireGuard-based products, and comparable private-network/overlay systems may be used as feature, interoperability, deployment, usability, and migration references. GoreeCloud Network must retain its own product architecture, control contracts, platform integrations, and evidence standards.

## Product objectives

GoreeCloud Network / Conduit should compete through:

- first-party GoreeCloud network capability ownership;
- safe capability-by-capability migration instead of opaque authority transfer;
- strong private connectivity and device-network experience;
- explicit transport identity and resilient relay behavior;
- obfuscation options with no silent downgrade when required;
- privacy-minimized status and evidence surfaces;
- clear Identity, security, privacy, continuity, DNS, Gateway, and Mesh responsibility boundaries;
- accessible current-Stable Glaze UI administration where user-facing control is required;
- portable backup, recovery, and succession through Everkeep;
- verifiable exact-source/artifact/deployment provenance.

## Engineering objectives before production-native authority

1. Complete state migration, backup/restore, and rollback evidence for each migrated capability.
2. Retain final immutable staging/container image identities.
3. Complete Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, Mesh, Identity, and governance evidence production/consumption in the target environment.
4. Complete representative restrictive-network and obfuscation acceptance.
5. Produce source-bound signed Android artifacts and approved real-device evidence where applicable.
6. Validate failure, reconnect, relay, routing, DNS interaction, and upgrade behavior without silent authority changes.
7. Maintain privacy-safe observability and incident evidence.
8. Rehearse reversible production migration before removing compatibility authority.
9. Keep production cutover as an explicit operational approval outside source-level contracts.

## Differentiation rule

Competitive claims must identify the evidence-bearing implemented capability. Planned native authority, production scale, target-environment platform acceptance, real-device acceptance, and completed NetBird replacement remain objectives until the corresponding evidence exists.
